title: 'AI accounts'
description: 'Sign in your Claude and ChatGPT plans, stack several, and see what happens when one runs out.'

Teammates run on your own Claude plan (for Claude Code) or ChatGPT plan (for Codex). Brigade never bills you for AI
usage and never uses a pay-per-use API key. You manage your plans in **Settings → AI Accounts**.

## Add an account

Open **Settings → AI Accounts** and choose **Add Claude account** or **Add Codex account**. If more than one computer
is online, pick the one to sign in on. A computer must be online to add an account.

**Claude:**

1. Choose **Open Claude sign-in** and sign in to the Claude account you want to add.
2. Claude shows a code. Paste it in the box and choose **Finish**.

On the cloud computer, Claude doesn't tell Brigade which account you signed in to. Fill in **Account email
(optional)** to tell your accounts apart.

**Codex:**

1. Open the address shown and sign in to ChatGPT.
2. Enter the code shown on the page. The page updates when you are done.

You sign in on the vendor's own page: Brigade never sees your password, and the login stays on the computer. See
[Security and your data](/security#what-brigade-stores) for how cloud computer logins are kept.

### A login you already have on your computer

On your own computer, a `claude` or `codex` login you already have in the terminal shows up by itself, as
**Claude on** or **Codex on** followed by the machine's name. It reads **Uses this machine's own login**. Removing it
from Brigade leaves the login on your machine.

The runner removes API keys such as `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` and `OPENAI_API_KEY` from what teammates see, so work always
runs on your plan.

## Who uses an account

An account lives on one computer, and a thread uses only accounts on the computer it runs on.

- **On the cloud computer, accounts are the workspace's.** Every member's threads, routines and triggers there run on
  them, whoever added them. Each shows who added it. Only that member changes it; they, an owner or an admin can
  remove it.
- **On your own computer, accounts are yours alone.** Only your threads use them, and other members can't see them.

A member who joins a workspace whose cloud computer has an account starts threads right away, without adding one.
When a member also has an account of their own on the cloud computer, their threads use theirs first.

## Several accounts

Add as many accounts of each kind as you like. The first one you add for Claude or Codex is your **Default**; change
it with **Make default** in an account's menu.

When an account runs out of usage in the middle of a turn, the thread carries on with your next account **of the same
kind** on the same computer: Claude to Claude, Codex to Codex. The thread notes the switch, and the teammate picks up
from a summary of the thread so far. It never switches from Claude to Codex or back.

Brigade tries your accounts in this order: the one picked for the thread, your default, then the oldest. Accounts
out of usage go last, the one that resets soonest first.

### When every account is out

If none of your accounts of that kind has usage left, the thread pauses and opens a ticket under
[Needs you](/needs-you): **Paused: every account has run out of usage**, with the time usage resets. To go on:

- wait for the reset, then send a message in the thread,
- or upgrade your plan or add an account, then send a message.

Sending a message closes the ticket. If you know usage is back before Brigade does, choose **Usage is back** in the
account's menu.

## Pick an account for a thread

In a new thread, the composer's **+** menu has an **Account** setting when you have more than one usable account for
that teammate on that computer. Pick one and it shows as a chip in the composer; remove the chip to go back to your
default. Without a pick, the thread uses your default.

A thread that has started has no account picker: it changes account only when one runs out.

## See your usage

Each account on **Settings → AI Accounts** shows its status (**Ready**, **Sign-in needed**, **Out of usage until** a
time) and meters for its limits: **5h**, **7d**, and **7d Opus** or **7d Sonnet** when the plan reports them. Hover a meter for the
percentage used and when it resets. A meter turns amber from 80%.

The **Usage** row in your avatar menu shows the same at a glance, with **See what used it** and **Manage accounts**.
Usage appears after a teammate's next turn. See [Usage and limits](/usage-and-limits).

## Models and accounts

Each teammate has a model, picked on its **Profile** tab: a Claude model runs on a Claude account, a Codex model on a
ChatGPT account. **Default** uses the account's own default model for Claude. If you have no ready account of the
teammate's kind on the computer, the composer says so with a link to add one. See [Teammates](/teammates#models).

## Manage accounts

Each account's menu has:

- **Make default**: try this account first.
- **Usage is back**: clear an out-of-usage mark.
- **Sign in again**: when its login stopped working. Brigade opens a **Sign in again** ticket after two failed turns
  in a row.
- **Remove**: deletes the login from the computer, which must be online.
