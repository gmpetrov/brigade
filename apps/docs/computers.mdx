A teammate works on a computer: it runs the real Claude Code or Codex there, with a browser, your repositories and
the previews of the apps it builds. The computer is linked to your workspace by a small program, the **runner**.
You can use your own computer for free, Brigade's cloud computer, or both.

|                                 | Your own computer                   | Brigade Cloud                                        |
| ------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| **Price**                       | Free                                | $99 a month per workspace (€99 in the euro zone)     |
| **When teammates work**         | While your computer is on and awake | All the time, even when your laptop is closed        |
| **Routines and triggers**       | Only while your computer is on      | On time, even at 3 a.m.                              |
| **AI usage**                    | Your Claude or ChatGPT plan         | Your Claude or ChatGPT plan                          |
| **Systems**                     | macOS, Linux                        | Nothing to install                                   |
| **Computer use**                | Any app on your Mac                 | Its own browser; you can take control of its desktop |
| **Take control, Teach a task**  | Not yet                             | Yes                                                  |
| **Website logins from Secrets** | Not yet                             | Yes                                                  |
| **Turns at once**               | Up to 8 by default                  | Up to 8, shared by the workspace                     |

Teammates, threads and members are unlimited in both.

## Your own computer

### On a Mac: Brigade.app

1. In onboarding, or in **Settings → Computers**, choose **Download Brigade for Mac** (macOS 14 or later).
2. Open it, then choose **Open in Brigade** on the page. Brigade.app asks before it installs anything, links the Mac
   to your workspace and keeps the runner going. It lives in the menu bar and updates itself.
3. Follow **Set up Brigade…** in its menu: move it to Applications, open it at login, and, for computer use, allow
   Screen Recording and Accessibility.

**Computer use.** On your Mac, teammates can read the screen, click and type in any app. macOS gives those
permissions to a separate helper, **Brigade Computer Use**, not to the teammate. While a teammate acts, a pill on
the screen shows its name and a **Stop** button.

<Tip>
  Teammates work only while your Mac is awake. **Keep screen awake** in Brigade's menu stops it from
  sleeping.
</Tip>

### On Linux, or a Mac from the terminal

In **Settings → Computers**, choose **Link your machine** and run the command it shows. It looks like this:

```bash
curl -fsSL https://api.brigade.lol/runner/install.sh | sh \
  && ~/.brigade/bin/brigade-runner link <CODE> --api https://api.brigade.lol \
  && ~/.brigade/bin/brigade-runner start
```

- It needs `curl` and `tar`, and no root access. It installs Node.js 24 if the machine has no recent one, and puts
  everything in `~/.brigade`.
- It works on Linux (x86_64, arm64) and macOS (Apple silicon, Intel). Windows isn't supported.
- The code works once, for 10 minutes. Linking the same machine again replaces it rather than adding a second one.
- Only one runner runs per machine.

### Your AI login stays on your computer

Sign in with **Sign in with Claude** or **Sign in with ChatGPT (Codex)** on the computer's card, or keep the `claude`
or `codex` login you already have in your terminal: it shows up by itself. The runner removes API keys such as
`ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` and `OPENAI_API_KEY` from what the teammate sees, so the work always runs on your plan, never on
a key that bills per use.

A computer you link is yours alone: other members of the workspace can't start threads on it.

## Brigade Cloud

Subscribe in **Settings → Billing → Subscribe** (owners and admins), or with **Get Brigade Cloud** in onboarding.
Payment goes through Stripe. The cloud computer appears in **Settings → Computers** within a few minutes; sign it in
to your Claude or ChatGPT account there.

- **Always on, when needed.** It sleeps after 30 idle minutes and wakes on the next message, routine or trigger.
- **Shared by the workspace.** Every member's threads can run on it, each on that member's own AI accounts.
- **Teammates kept apart.** Each teammate runs as its own Linux user with a private home folder, and each thread has
  its own network, temporary files and processes. Each teammate has its own Docker, without root.
- **Backed up.** Every 10 minutes and before each sleep: thread folders (encrypted), each teammate's scheduled jobs
  and services, and browser sign-ins. **Replace cloud computer** in its menu builds a new one and restores all of
  it. Docker images, previews and tool caches are not backed up.
- **Take control.** Open the computer's desktop in a thread and choose **Take control** to use it yourself, for
  example to solve a CAPTCHA. The teammate waits; choose **Hand back**, with an optional note, when you are done.
- **Teach a task.** In the composer's **+** menu, record yourself doing a task on the cloud computer (up to 10
  minutes, in Chrome, Edge or Firefox on a desktop). The teammate writes the steps down as a skill, and does the task
  on its own once an admin adds it.

### Cancel

Choose **Manage billing** in **Settings → Billing** to update your card, read invoices or cancel. Cloud stops at the
end of the month you paid for. Your workspace stays; the cloud computer stops but keeps its disk until you subscribe
again, and your teammates can keep working for free on your own computer.

## Move a thread between computers

Pick the computer when you start a thread (**Run on**). To move one later, choose **Continue on…** in the composer's
**+** menu. When a thread's computer is offline, the thread offers **Continue on the cloud computer**.

<Note>
  A thread picks up on another computer from what is on GitHub and in the thread. Files that exist
  only on the first computer stay there, so commit and push what matters.
</Note>
