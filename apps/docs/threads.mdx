---
title: 'Threads'
description: 'Start a thread, write to a teammate, follow its work, and stop, move or organize threads.'
---

A thread is one conversation with one teammate. You write what you need, and the thread shows everything the teammate
does about it, step by step, until it is done or needs you.

## Start a thread

Choose **New thread**, the round **+** at the top of the sidebar, and write what the teammate should work on. You can
also start one from Home, from a [project](/projects), or from anywhere with **⌘⇧K** (Ctrl+Shift+K), which opens a
quick box for a teammate and project.

Before you send, you can set:

- **The teammate**: on Home and in the quick box, under **Send to**.
- **Project**: **No project**, or one of your projects. The thread gets the project's brief and memory.
- In the **+** menu, **Run on**: which [computer](/computers) runs it. Shown when more than one is online.
- In the **+** menu, **Account**: which of your [AI accounts](/ai-accounts) it uses. Shown when you have more than one
  that fits.

A pick other than the default shows as a chip in the composer; its × goes back to the default.

## Write to a teammate

**Enter** sends, **Shift+Enter** adds a line. While the teammate works, Enter queues your message for after its turn.
A queued message has **Steer**, to give it to the teammate now, during the turn, and a menu with **Run in parallel**
(the teammate starts a child thread for it), **Edit** and **Remove**.

### Attach files

Choose **Attach files** in the **+** menu, drag files onto the thread, or paste them. Up to 10 files per message, 25
MB per file (500 MB for a video), 50 MB in all, not counting videos.

### Mention with @

Type **@** to mention:

- **Teammates**: brings them into the thread.
- **Projects**, **Apps**, **Repositories**, **Designs**, **Design systems**, **Library** files, **Threads** and
  **Computers**: points the teammate at them.
- **Secrets**: lets the teammate use that secret in this thread. See [Secrets](/apps#secrets).

### The + menu

- **Teach a task**: record yourself doing a task on the cloud computer so the teammate learns it as a skill. Cloud
  computer only. See [Brigade Cloud](/computers#brigade-cloud).
- **Continue on…**: move the thread to another computer (see below).
- **Effort**: how hard the teammate thinks, from Low to Max.

Type **/** at the start of a message for commands: **/visual**, **/design**, and your workspace's
[skills](/skills) by name.

## What a thread shows

- **Steps.** Each command run, file read or edited, search and browser action is a line you can open. Steps in a row
  fold into one line, such as "Ran 3 commands, read 2 files". A turn longer than 30 seconds folds under
  **Worked for** its duration. While it works, the thread shows how many steps and how long.
- **Cards.** Plans, approvals, questions, pull requests, and a line for each child thread started.
- **Tickets.** What the teammate is waiting on shows in a card above the conversation, and under
  [Needs you](/needs-you).
- **Details.** Choose the title at the top to open the details panel: the computer's live screen (cloud computer
  only), the thread's menu, its child threads, and its **Outputs**, including the changed files. Choose a changed
  file to see the diff.
- **Preview.** The eye button at top right starts or opens the preview of an app the teammate builds. See
  [Code and previews](/code-and-previews).

## Statuses and filters

A dot beside each thread in the sidebar shows where it stands:

| Dot          | Meaning                                   |
| ------------ | ----------------------------------------- |
| Violet       | Working                                   |
| Orange       | Needs you: waiting on a person, or paused |
| Red          | Failed, or updates stopped arriving       |
| Grey, filled | Idle or done, unread                      |
| Grey ring    | Idle or done, read                        |

Threads that need you come first, then working ones, then the rest. **Filter threads**, next to **Threads**, filters
by **Status** (**Needs you**, **Working**, **Unread**, **Failed**), by teammate and by project, and shows threads you
put off with **Do later** and **Archived** ones.

## Stop a run

While a teammate works, a round stop button appears in the composer, labeled **Stop** and the teammate's name. Choose
it to end the turn. The thread shows **Interrupted**, and any queued messages run next.

<Note>
  **Esc** never stops a teammate. It closes menus and panels, so pressing it by habit can't cut a
  run short.
</Note>

## Answer a teammate

A teammate can end its reply with up to three buttons, such as "Send it" or "Not yet". Choosing one sends its text as
your message. Two buttons do more:

- **Merge** merges the pull request the teammate opened. Owners and admins only, while it can merge.
- **Preview** starts the app's preview without a new turn, while none runs.

When a teammate offers options, it shows a card with 2 to 6 of them. Choose **Pick this one**, or write what you want
instead.

## Child threads

A teammate can hand part of the work to a child thread, for another teammate or to work in parallel. **Run in
parallel** on a queued message makes one too. Child threads sit folded under their parent in the
sidebar; open them with the arrow after its title. The parent shows when a child needs you, and the child's last
message goes back to the parent.

## Move a thread to another computer

Choose **Continue on…** in the **+** menu and pick a computer. The next message you send moves the thread; you can send
it empty. If your machine is offline, the thread offers **Continue on the cloud computer** or **Wait**. Only the member
who started the thread can move it.

The thread picks up from what is on GitHub and in the conversation. Files that exist only on the first computer stay
there. See [Your computer or Brigade Cloud](/computers#move-a-thread-between-computers).

## Who sees a thread

Every member of the workspace sees every thread and can watch it live. Only the member who started a thread writes in
it, because it runs on their AI subscription: others see that it runs on that member's subscription. The member who
started it also answers its approvals and questions; a few approvals wait for an owner or admin instead.

## Organize threads

The **⋮** menu on a thread has:

**Pin**, **Do later** (puts it aside, for you only), **Rename**, **Archive**, and **Delete**, which removes it for
good with its files (the member who started it, or an admin).
