---
title: 'Members and roles'
description: 'Who can do what in your organization, what stays personal, and how to delete a workspace or your account.'
---

People belong to an **organization**, the account you created at sign-up. Each member has one role, which applies in
every workspace of the organization: **owner**, **admin** or **member**. See [Key concepts](/concepts) for how
organizations, workspaces and projects fit together.

<Note>The dashboard doesn't have a screen to invite people or change their role yet.</Note>

## What each role can do

| Role       | Can                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ---------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Member** | Start threads, connect their own AI accounts, Apps and computers, add secrets, create routines and projects, answer tickets on their own threads                                                                                 |
| **Admin**  | All of that, plus: create workspaces and teammates, edit teammates' access, policies and caps, manage billing, the cloud computer, skills, triggers, email and the Security activity log, merge pull requests, answer any ticket |
| **Owner**  | All of that, plus: delete a workspace                                                                                                                                                                                            |

Some things belong to the member who made them, whatever their role: an App account they connected, a secret they
created, a routine they set up. They, or an owner or admin, manage it.

## What stays personal

- **AI accounts** are yours alone. A thread runs on the accounts of the member who started it, and nobody else's
  threads use them. See [AI accounts](/ai-accounts).
- **Your own computer** is listed as **only you** in **Settings → Computers**: only you run threads on it. The cloud
  computer is **shared** by the workspace.
- **App accounts**: personal Apps (Gmail, Calendar, Notion…) start as **Only in my threads**. Switch one to **In
  every thread** to share it. See [Apps](/apps#connect-an-app).
- **Threads**: every member sees every thread of the workspace, but only the member who started one writes in it.
  Others see it live.

## Workspaces

A workspace is one business or product line, with its own teammates, computer, Apps, secrets, library, memory and
billing. Switch between them from the workspace name at the top of the sidebar. Owners and admins create one with
**New workspace**.

## Delete your account

**Settings → Preferences → Delete account**, then type your email to confirm. Brigade deletes:

- organizations where you are the only member, with all their workspaces,
- in shared organizations: the threads you started and their files, your routines, tasks, computers, AI accounts and
  the Apps you kept to your own threads,
- your sign-ins and sessions.

It is refused while a teammate is working in one of your threads, while a workspace only you belong to still has
Brigade Cloud (cancel it first), or when you are the only owner of a shared organization.

## Delete a workspace

Owners only: **Settings → General → Delete workspace**, then type the workspace's name. Brigade deletes its threads,
tasks, routines and files, its library, memory, skills and projects, its teammates and their email addresses, its Apps
(disconnected at the vendor) and secrets, its cloud computer with its disk, and its email domains.

It is refused while threads are running, while the cloud computer is being created or replaced, or while Brigade
Cloud is active: cancel it in **Settings → Billing** first. Members' own AI account logins stay on their computers.
See [Security and your data](/security#delete-your-data) for how long backups last.
