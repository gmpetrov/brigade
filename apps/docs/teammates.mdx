A teammate is the real Claude Code or Codex, given a job. It has a name, role instructions, a model, and the Apps,
Secrets and library access you choose. It works on a [computer](/computers), on your own
[AI account](/ai-accounts), and every message to it opens or continues a [thread](/threads).

## Chief

Every workspace starts with a teammate called **Chief**, created with the workspace. Chief is your chief of staff and
the first teammate you talk to. It has two jobs:

- **Set up the team.** Chief asks what you're building, what takes your time and which tools the work lives in, one
  or two questions at a time. Then it proposes a team, usually two or three teammates, each with a role,
  instructions and the Apps it needs.
- **Answer and route.** Chief answers simple questions itself. For real work, it hands off to the right teammate: it
  mentions them, starts a sub-thread, or creates a task. It doesn't do a teammate's job itself.
- **Answer your teammates for you.** When a teammate ends its reply on buttons or choices, Chief reads the question
  before you do, and suggests an answer or gives it in your name. See below.

Chief's name can't change, and it can't be archived.

### Chief answers for you

When a teammate ends its reply on buttons or choices in one of your threads, Chief reads the question first:

- **It suggests** the answer it would pick, under the question. Choose **Use it** to send it as yours, or answer as
  you like.
- **It leaves the question to you** when it's a matter of taste, anything that leaves Brigade (sending, publishing,
  merging, paying), or nothing settles it, and says why.
- **It answers on its own** only for the kinds of question you let it: next steps, approaches and priorities. Its
  answer shows in the thread as **Chief answered for you**, with why. **Mark as wrong** tells the teammate to stop and
  puts that kind back to suggestions. Three answers in a row in a thread without a word from you, and the next
  question waits for you.

Chief decides from your **mandate**, `chief/mandate.md` in the library: what you aim for, the decisions that hold, and
what you always decide yourself. Chief writes it with you when it sets up your team, or from **Write it with Chief** in
**Preferences**, and can look back with you each week.

Once you took Chief's pick 9 of your last 10 times for a kind of question, a ticket offers to let it answer that kind
on its own. Switch each kind yourself in **Preferences → Chief**, or turn Chief's answers off: then every question
waits for you.

Chief's answer is never your approval. While a teammate acts on it, its App actions ask you first, except opening a
branch or a pull request. On its computer a teammate still runs commands without asking: it's told not to deploy,
publish or send that way on Chief's word alone, but Brigade can't hold those back.

### The team proposal

Chief's proposal shows as a card titled **Your team**. For each teammate you can choose **Edit** to change its
**Name** and **Instructions**, or leave it out with the X. Under **Apps this team needs**, choose **Connect** for each
App you haven't connected yet; you can also create the team first and connect them after.

Choose **Create team** to create them (owners and admins). Each new teammate gets access to the Apps it needs that are
already connected, and its writes to Apps ask a person first. Change that on its **Access** tab. For an App you
connect later, the card shows a **Give** button to grant it. Or write to Chief what to change.

## Create a teammate

Owners and admins choose **New teammate**: the **+** next to **Teammates** in the sidebar. The form asks for:

| Field                 | What to put                                                                     |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Name**              | Up to 80 characters.                                                            |
| **Title**             | Optional, up to 40 characters. Shown as a badge beside its name.                |
| **Role instructions** | Its job, in your words. Optional, but a teammate without them is a blank hire.  |
| **Model**             | One list, grouped by **Claude** and **Codex**. Picking a model picks the agent. |

Then choose **Create teammate**. The avatar is generated for you.

### Models

| Claude (Claude Code)                                       | Codex                                                                                     |
| ---------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Default**: the default model of the account it runs on   | **Default**: GPT-6.1 Sol                                                                  |
| **Opus 5.5**, **Fable 5.1**, **Sonnet 5.5**, **Haiku 5.5** | **GPT-6.1 Sol**, **GPT-6 Astra**, **GPT-6 Sol**, **GPT-6 Luna**, and older GPT-5.6 models |

A Claude teammate runs on a Claude account, a Codex teammate on a ChatGPT account. Its threads need an account of
that kind on the computer they run on. See [AI accounts](/ai-accounts).

<Warning>
  Codex can't ask before running commands or editing files, so a Codex teammate's threads run them
  without asking.
</Warning>

## Write good role instructions

Write to the teammate as "you", like a brief for a new hire:

- **Start with the role.** The first line says what it does, for example "You handle customer support for Acme."
  Chief reads that line to decide what work to send it.
- **Say what it owns**: the inbox, the repository, the weekly report.
- **Say how it works**: tone, where the answers are, what good looks like.
- **Say what to ask a person about first**: refunds, anything sent to a customer, merging.

Facts that change belong in [memory](/memory), and repeatable procedures in [skills](/skills).

## The teammate page

Open a teammate from the sidebar. Its page has four tabs.

### Profile

The same fields as the creation form, with **Save**, and **Archive**. Only owners and admins see this tab.

### Access

What the teammate may reach:

- **Document library**: **Read only** (the default) or **Read and write**. Folders where its access differs, set in
  the [library](/library), are listed under it.
- **Mailbox**: its own email address, if your workspace has the [Email App](/email).
- **Apps**: one row per connected account, **No access**, **Read only** or **Read and write**. A personal App
  shows whose threads it works in.
- **Secrets**: **No access**, **Can use, asks first** or **Without asking** for each.
- **Acts without approval (sending, editing, deleting)**: the policy for writes to Apps. **Allow** (the default),
  **Ask a person first**, or **Never**. See [Apps](/apps#read-write-and-approvals).
- **Starts threads from**: the [triggers](/triggers) that start threads for this teammate.

### Activity

A timeline of the teammate's threads over the **Last hour**, **Last 8 hours**, **Last 24 hours** or **Last 7 days**,
with totals for **Working**, **Waiting for approval** and **Blocked**.

### Limits

**Daily caps**: **Threads started**, **Computer hours** and **Write calls per connection**. Reaching a cap pauses the
teammate's new work and opens a ticket for an admin. Days start at midnight UTC; an empty field means no cap. Owners
and admins choose **Edit caps**. See [Usage and limits](/usage-and-limits).

## Archive a teammate

On the **Profile** tab, choose **Archive**. Its threads stay. Its triggers are deleted, its routines stop running,
and it can no longer be mentioned. There is no way to bring an archived teammate back yet, so create a new one if you
need it again.

## Who may do what

| Action                                                             | Who                                               |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------- |
| Create teammates, **Create team**, edit a profile, archive         | Owners and admins                                 |
| Library access, write policy, daily caps                           | Owners and admins                                 |
| Give a teammate an App account                                     | Owners and admins, or the member who connected it |
| Give a teammate a Secret                                           | Owners and admins, or the member who saved it     |
| See **Access**, **Activity** and **Limits**; start threads with it | Everyone                                          |

See [Members](/members) for roles.
