You need a **Claude** plan (for Claude Code) or a **ChatGPT** plan (for Codex). Brigade runs your teammates on the
plan you already pay for and never bills you for AI usage. Max or Pro gives the most room.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Go to [brigade.lol/sign-up](https://www.brigade.lol/sign-up) and choose **Continue with Google**, or enter your
    name and email and choose **Email me a code**. The code works for 10 minutes. There is no password to remember.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name what you are building">
    Onboarding asks **What are you building?** Enter a project name and choose **Continue**. This creates your
    workspace: its teammates, threads, files and memory are kept apart from any other workspace you make later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give your teammates a computer">
    Teammates work on a computer, with the real Claude Code or Codex. Pick one or both:

    - **Brigade Cloud** ($99 a month per workspace): a computer of their own that keeps working when yours is off.
      Choose **Get Brigade Cloud**; it is ready in a few minutes.
    - **Your own computer** (free): on a Mac, choose **Download Brigade for Mac**, open it, then **Open in Brigade**.
      On Linux, or on a Mac from a terminal, run the install command shown on the page.

    See [Your computer or Brigade Cloud](/computers) to choose.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your AI account">
    Once the computer is online, choose **Sign in with Claude** or **Sign in with ChatGPT (Codex)**. You sign in on
    the vendor's own page; Brigade never sees your password.

    - **Claude**: choose **Open Claude sign-in**, sign in, then paste the code Claude shows you and choose **Finish**.
    - **Codex**: open the address shown and enter the code.

    On your own computer, a `claude` or `codex` login you already have in the terminal shows up by itself.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Meet Chief">
    Every workspace starts with a teammate called **Chief**. Your first thread with Chief opens by itself: tell it
    what your business does and what takes your time. Chief proposes a team, with the Apps each teammate needs, and
    creates it when you choose **Create team**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Your first piece of work

Open a teammate and write to it in a thread, the way you would to a new hire:

> Sort this week's support email and draft answers to the questions our docs already cover.

The thread shows everything the teammate does, step by step: what it read, ran and changed. When it needs you (an
approval, a question, an account it can't reach), the thread shows up under **Needs you** in the sidebar and in
**Waiting on you** on Home, and you get a push on your phone if you turned them on.

<Tip>
  Install the dashboard on your phone (Share → **Add to Home Screen** on iPhone, iOS 16.4 or later)
  to get a push when a teammate needs you. Choose which pushes you get in **Settings → Preferences →
  Notifications**.
</Tip>

## Create a teammate yourself

In the sidebar's **Teammates** section, choose **New teammate** (owners and admins). Give it:

- a **Name** and a **Title**, shown as a badge beside its name,
- **Role instructions**: its job, in your words,
- a **Model**: Claude Code (Opus, Sonnet, Haiku…) or Codex.

Then, on the teammate's **Access** tab, choose the [Apps](/apps) and secrets it may use, and what it may do without
asking. On **Limits**, set daily caps on the threads it starts, its computer hours and its writes to each App.
