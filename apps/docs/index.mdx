Brigade gives you a team of AI teammates. They write your code, answer your customers, handle your marketing and
chase your leads. Every decision that needs you lands in one list, on the web or on your phone.

Each teammate is the real **Claude Code** or **Codex**, signed in with your own account, with a job, a computer to
work on, the Apps you give it and a memory of how your team works. You talk to it in threads, the way you would with
a new hire.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a teammate">
    Give it a name, a title, its role and a model (Claude Code or Codex), then the Apps it may use.
    For example: _Sam, support. Gmail and your Notion help center. On your Claude Max plan._
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give it a job">
    Write to it in a thread: _"Sort every support email and draft the answers our docs already
    cover."_
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve what matters">
    Sam wants to send a reply to a customer. You read it and tap **Allow**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Three pieces work together:

- **The dashboard** at [www.brigade.lol](https://www.brigade.lol) holds your workspace: teammates, threads, Needs
  you, tasks, the library, memory, the vault and the Apps.
- **A computer** runs each thread with the real Claude Code or Codex CLI, signed in with your account. It is your own
  Mac or Linux machine, for free, or a cloud computer with [Brigade Cloud](/computers).
- **Apps** reach your teammates as tools. Writes can wait for your yes, and every call is logged in the thread.

## Brigade is open source

The whole product is on [GitHub](https://github.com/gmpetrov/brigade) under the MIT license. You can read every line
of the software that holds your passwords and your customers' email, and run it yourself.

Not sure yet? [Click around the live demo](https://demo.brigade.lol): it is the real dashboard, on a sample
workspace.
