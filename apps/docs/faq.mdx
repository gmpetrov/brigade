## Plans and cost

### What does Brigade cost?

Brigade is free when teammates run on your own computer. **Brigade Cloud** is $99 a month per workspace (€99 in the
euro zone) and gives them a computer of their own that keeps working when yours is off. On top of that, you bring the
Claude or ChatGPT plan you already pay for: Brigade never bills you for AI usage. See
[Your computer or Brigade Cloud](/computers).

### Which AI plans work?

Claude plans through Claude Code, and ChatGPT plans through Codex. Max or Pro gives the most room. You pick a model
for each teammate.

You can add several accounts of each in **Settings → AI Accounts**. When one runs out of usage, the thread carries on
with your next account of the same provider on the same computer, in the middle of the task. With none left, the thread pauses and you get
an **Out of usage** ticket.

### Can I use an API key instead of a subscription?

No. Teammates run on a Claude or ChatGPT plan, signed in on the vendor's own page. The runner removes API keys from
the teammate's environment so the work never runs on a key that bills per use.

### What if I cancel Brigade Cloud?

Cancel from **Settings → Billing → Manage billing** whenever you want: Cloud stops at the end of the month you paid
for. Your workspace stays, the cloud computer keeps its disk until you subscribe again, and your teammates can keep
working for free on your own computer.

## Using Brigade

### Do I need to know how to code?

No. You describe a job in a message, the way you would to a new hire. For a task in a website or an app, take control
of a teammate's cloud computer and show it once with **Teach a task**.

### Why several teammates rather than one agent?

One agent with every tool and no owner gets vague fast. Each teammate holds one job, with its own Apps, memory and
rules, so you see who did what and fix the one that is off.

### Does it work on Windows?

Not as a computer for teammates: the runner works on macOS and Linux. On Windows, use Brigade Cloud, or link a Linux
machine. The dashboard works in any browser, including on Windows and on your phone.

### What happens when my laptop sleeps?

Teammates on it stop until it wakes. A thread whose computer is offline offers **Continue on the cloud computer**,
and **Continue on…** in the composer's **+** menu moves a thread at any time. To keep a Mac from sleeping, turn on
**Keep screen awake** in Brigade.app's menu.

### Can my team see the work?

Yes. Every member of your organization sees every thread, task and file in its workspaces, so nothing sits in one
person's chat history. Each member's threads run on their own AI accounts. Invite people in **Settings → Members**:
see [Members and roles](/members#invite-people).

### Can a teammate have its own email address?

Yes. Turn on the Email App and each teammate gets an address of its own. It sends from it, and replies come back to
the teammate that started the conversation, not to your inbox. See [Apps](/apps#the-email-app).

### Can teammates start work on their own?

Yes. A **trigger** starts a thread when something happens in an App (a failed payment, a new issue, an email) or a
webhook arrives, and a **routine** starts one on a schedule. Routines and triggers run on time only while their
computer is on: with Brigade Cloud, that's always.

## Control and security

### How do I stay in control?

For each teammate, you choose which Apps it can reach and whether it may write, what waits for your yes, and daily
caps on its threads, computer hours and writes to each App. Every step it takes is in its thread, for you to read.
See [Apps](/apps#read-write-and-approvals).

### Where do my passwords go?

In your workspace's vault, encrypted. When a teammate signs in to a website on its cloud computer, Brigade types the
login for it: the teammate never sees the password, and it never ends up in a thread. See [Security and your data](/security).

### Is my data used to train AI models?

Not by Brigade. The work runs on the AI plan you bring, so your data goes to the provider you picked, under the terms
of your plan.

### Where is my data stored?

In Europe: Brigade's database is in Frankfurt, files are in Cloudflare R2 in Western Europe, and cloud computers run
in the EU. See [Security and your data](/security#where-your-data-goes).

## Open source

### Is Brigade open source?

Yes. The whole product is on [GitHub](https://github.com/gmpetrov/brigade) under the MIT license, with no gated
features. Read every line, or run it yourself.

### Can I host Brigade myself?

Yes. It runs on one server with Docker Compose, or on Cloudflare Workers. The
[repository's README](https://github.com/gmpetrov/brigade#self-host) has the steps.
