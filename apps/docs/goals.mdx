---
title: 'Goals'
description: 'Have a teammate work until a goal is reached, measured by Brigade rather than by its own word.'
---

Start a message with **/goal** and say what you want reached: "tests green on the new API", "10 demos booked
by Friday", "+100 GitHub stars today". The teammate keeps working until Brigade's measurement says the goal is
reached, or until its deadline, its budget or its progress says to stop.

## The contract

The teammate first turns your goal into a contract and shows it at the top of the thread:

- **Result and deadline**: what you expect, and by when.
- **Target**: where the measurement must land, such as at least 10.
- **Measured by**: a command such as your tests, a read of an [App](/apps) the teammate already has (Stripe,
  PostHog, GitHub, Search Console, Google Calendar…), the ticked lines of a [list](/lists), or, when no figure
  is possible, your own judgment at the deadline.
- **Allowed**: the writes it may make without asking you, each one precise action with a cap, such as "Gmail:
  send, 20 a day". Anything past the cap or outside the list asks you as usual.
- **Budget**: how much of your Claude or Codex subscription it may spend, sub-threads included, and the longest
  it runs.

Choose **Approve and start**, or write what to change and the teammate proposes it again. Nothing starts
before you approve.

## How it runs

- **Brigade measures, not the teammate.** At the end of each turn, and every so often for an App or a list,
  Brigade runs the contract's measurement. The goal is reached only when the measurement says so; the
  teammate cannot declare it done before.
- **It keeps going.** When a turn ends with nothing open and the goal not reached, Brigade prompts the teammate
  again with the latest measurement and what remains.
- **It waits like any thread.** While a ticket, a sub-thread, a review, a pull request or a wake-up is open, the
  thread sleeps. If the measurement moves away from the target meanwhile, the teammate is woken to adjust.
- **It stops on its own** at the deadline, when the budget is spent, or when nothing moves for several turns
  in a row, and the teammate writes a summary: the final measurement, what worked and what did nothing.
- **It leaves room for your other threads.** When your subscription's usage window is nearly spent, the goal
  waits for it to reset.
- **Your message comes first.** A message you send cancels the next automatic prompt that has not gone yet.

## The banner

The banner at the top of the thread shows the measurement and its curve, the target, the deadline, the budget
spent and what is allowed, with each permission's use today. From it you can **Pause**, **Resume**, **Stop**,
or withdraw a permission at any time: its writes ask you again at once.

## What a goal never does

Whatever the contract says, a teammate working toward a goal follows each platform's rules, never uses fake
accounts or poses as an ordinary user to promote something, never fabricates stars, votes or reviews, says it
is an AI when it talks with people, and leaves to you, in your own words, the posts where AI text is not
allowed.
