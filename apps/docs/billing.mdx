title: 'Billing'
description: 'What Brigade costs, what Brigade Cloud Starter and Max add, and how to subscribe, upgrade, manage or cancel.'
---

Brigade is free on your own computers. **Brigade Cloud** adds a cloud computer for your workspace, in two plans:
**Starter**, $15 a month per workspace (€15 in the euro zone) for 100 hours of cloud computer a month, and **Max**,
$99 a month per workspace (€99) with no hour limit. There is one subscription per workspace, paid through Stripe.

## Free, Starter or Max

|                         | Free                                | Starter                                         | Max                                             |
| ----------------------- | ----------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| **Price**               | Free                                | $15 a month per workspace (€15)                 | $99 a month per workspace (€99)                 |
| **Where teammates run** | Your own computers                  | Brigade's cloud computer, and your own machines | Brigade's cloud computer, and your own machines |
| **Cloud computer**      | None                                | 100 hours a month                               | No hour limit                                   |
| **When teammates work** | While your computer is on and awake | Even when your laptop is closed                 | All the time                                    |
| **Email addresses**     | No                                  | One for each teammate                           | One for each teammate                           |
| **AI usage**            | Your Claude or ChatGPT plan         | Your Claude or ChatGPT plan                     | Your Claude or ChatGPT plan                     |

Max also comes with priority support. Brigade never bills you for AI usage, on any plan. See
[Your computer or Brigade Cloud](/computers) for what each computer can do.

## Starter's hours

An hour counts while the cloud computer is on. It goes to sleep 30 minutes after teammates finish, and wakes when
they have work. **Settings → Billing** shows the hours used this billing period and when they start over; from 80
hours, a card at the foot of the sidebar shows them too. Once the 100 hours are used, the cloud computer sleeps until
the next period, and teammates' cloud work waits for it. Email and the rest of your workspace keep working. To lift
the limit, choose **Upgrade to Max**: Stripe charges the difference for the rest of the period.

## Subscribe

Only owners and admins can subscribe or change the plan. Other members see the plan but no buttons.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Billing">
    Go to **Settings → Billing** and choose **Get Starter** or **Get Max**. In onboarding, **Start
    with Cloud** subscribes to Starter; you can move to Max later from **Settings → Billing**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay in Stripe Checkout">
    Checkout shows the price in your currency and accepts promotion codes and a tax ID.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in the cloud computer">
    Back in Brigade, the cloud computer appears in **Settings → Computers** within a few minutes.
    Sign it in to your Claude or ChatGPT account there.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The workspace is billed, not the person who pays: if the admin who subscribed leaves, the plan stays with the
workspace.

## Manage your plan

**Settings → Billing** shows the plan's state, such as **Renews on** a date or **Ends on** a date. Choose
**Manage billing** to open Stripe's billing portal, where you can:

- update your card
- read and download invoices
- cancel the plan

After the plan has ended, **Invoices** still opens the portal, and **Get Starter** or **Get Max** starts a new
plan.

## When a payment fails

The plan shows **Payment failed: update your card to keep it**. While Stripe retries the payment, your workspace
keeps the cloud computer. Update your card in **Manage billing**. If the payment still
fails and the plan ends, the cloud computer stops, as after a cancellation.

## Cancel

Cancel in **Manage billing**. The plan runs until the end of the period you paid for, then ends. Then:

- **The cloud computer stops** and keeps its disk. It doesn't start again until the workspace subscribes again.
- **Your workspace stays**: its threads, library, memory and members. Teammates keep working for free on your own
  computers. To move a thread there, see [Move a thread between computers](/computers#move-a-thread-between-computers).

<Note>
  You can't delete a workspace, or your account, while the workspace has Brigade Cloud. Cancel it
  first, and delete once the plan has ended.
</Note>

## Included by Brigade

Some workspaces get Brigade Cloud from the Brigade team at no charge. Their plan shows **Included by Brigade**, with
nothing to manage and no **Manage billing** button.

## Self-hosted Brigade

On a Brigade server you run yourself without Stripe set up, billing is off: **Settings → Billing** says so, and the
cloud computer, when the server has one, is included. See [Self-hosting](/self-hosting).
